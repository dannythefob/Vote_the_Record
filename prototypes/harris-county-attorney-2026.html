What this office does

The County Attorney is the county's chief civil lawyer. The office represents the county, its departments, and its elected and appointed officials in civil matters, and also represents the Harris County Hospital District and Flood Control District. It represents the State in mental health commitments and children's protective services cases. Its work includes lawsuits, contracts, land condemnation, legal opinions, collecting money owed to the county, and bond paperwork. The office has more than 300 attorneys and staff. Criminal prosecution belongs to the District Attorney, not this office. County Attorney's Office, Office profile, Ballotpedia

Why this matters

This office decides how the county defends itself when sued, advises officials before they act, and handles cases that affect children and people in mental health crises. Legal advice shapes what the county can do on contracts, flood rules and enforcement, and disputes with the state. It is not a policy-making seat, but its judgment calls affect taxpayer costs and county decisions.
