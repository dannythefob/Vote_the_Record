What this office does

The county judge presides over Commissioners Court and casts one of five votes. The court sets tax rates, approves budgets, calls bond elections, sets voting precinct lines, oversees jails and courthouses, and maintains roads, bridges and parks in unincorporated Harris County. KHOU

The judge also directs emergency management and oversees the county's Homeland Security and Emergency Management office. Texas Association of Counties, Harris County budget book

Limits: day-to-day county operations are run by a separate County Administration office created in 2021, and the judge cannot pass a budget or tax rate alone. The survey below only covers decisions this office can influence.
