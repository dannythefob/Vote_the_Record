About {{ config.site_name }}

A nonpartisan voter information project. It starts with Harris County, Texas, and is built to grow to every state.

What we publish

  • What each office can do, from the laws and official sources that define it.
  • What candidates have done: votes, sponsored measures, promises, and statements. Official government records come first: council and commissioners court minutes, legislative roll calls, election filings, and campaign finance reports.
  • What candidates are running on, sorted into policy proposals, attack messaging, and contested claims, with the same test applied to every candidate.

Our rules

  • Every fact links to its source and is labeled as an official record, a news report, or a candidate's own claim.
  • No claim appears without a source.
  • Survey scenarios are hypothetical and limited to what the office can actually do.
  • Party is shown only as it appears on the ballot. It is never used to sort, score, or color anything.
  • Candidates always appear in alphabetical order, never ranked by score.

How information is checked

Records are gathered by automated tools and research assistants, some of them AI. Everything starts as Unverified. A human reviewer checks each item against its source and an archived copy before marking it verified. Only verified records count toward match results.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party scripts or fonts. The survey runs entirely in your browser; your answers are never sent anywhere or saved.

Mistakes

If something is wrong, see Corrections. Every accepted correction is logged publicly.
