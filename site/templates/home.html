How it works

  1. Learn what the office can do. Each race page starts with the office's legal powers, so you know what the job does and doesn't cover.
  2. See the record. Votes, sponsored measures, promises, and statements, each labeled as an official record, a news report, or a candidate's own claim, with a link to the source.
  3. Answer scenarios. Hypothetical situations the office could actually face. Party is never mentioned.
  4. Compare. Your answers are matched against verified records in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere or saved.
