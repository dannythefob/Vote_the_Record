How we sort what candidates say
Policy proposal
A specific thing the candidate says they would do, with a source.
Attack messaging
Criticism of an opponent that names no policy and no record.
Contested claim
A disputed claim, shown with what is documented. We call a claim contradicted only when an official record or independent fact-check shows otherwise, and we link that evidence.

The same test is applied to every candidate. "Not found in the sources reviewed" means we haven't found one yet, not that none exists.

How quiz results work

We compare your answers with what each candidate has actually done. A vote counts more than a speech, and an official record counts more than a news story or a campaign claim. Only facts we've checked count.

If a candidate has no record on a question, it's left out, never counted against them. You'll only see a match percentage once there's enough checked record to compare. How it works, in full
