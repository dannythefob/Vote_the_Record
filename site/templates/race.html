How results are calculated

Each answer is compared with the candidate's records on that question. Votes count 3, sponsored measures 2, promises kept or broken 2, and statements 1, multiplied by an evidence factor (official record 1.0, news report 0.7, candidate's own claim 0.4) and by how much the question matters to you. Only verified, documented records count, and several records of the same event count once.

Questions where a candidate has no record are left out, never scored as zero. A percentage appears only when at least 3 of your answered questions have verified records; otherwise you see what is on record for each answer. Read the full methodology.
